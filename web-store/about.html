Our Story

Built by people who'd rather take the rooftops than the sidewalk.

Started On A Ledge, Not In A Boardroom

WEBSTRIKE started as a small print run of hoodies for a rooftop-running crew who wanted gear that actually looked like it belonged up there — heavyweight, weatherproof, and cut for movement, not a mall rack.

Every piece carries the same crimson-and-black signature and the spider-mark that's become our shorthand for "built for the climb." No licensed characters, no shortcuts — just original design, made for people who move through the city differently.
